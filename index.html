Server not showing up?

Work down the list — it's almost always one of these.

  • Check that you added youthgroup38 the way your device needs — not through PlayStation Network or the Switch friend list.
  • On PlayStation and Switch, make sure you're signed in to a Microsoft account inside Minecraft.
  • Close Minecraft completely, then open it again.
  • Check Play → Worlds again — the server shows up there as a friend’s world, not under Servers.
  • On Bedrock PC and mobile, try the other method — the address or the friends list.

Server rules

Short version: mess around, but don't ruin the server for everyone else.

01

Griefing

Griefing is allowed within reason.

Don't do anything extreme or destructive enough to ruin the server for everyone else — no lava casting, no wrecking large areas for no reason, no major damage to important community areas.

Keep world spawn and the area around it intact.

02

Stealing

Stealing and messing with people is allowed.

Don't repeatedly empty someone's entire base, destroy all of their progress, or target the same person over and over.

Use common sense and keep it fun.

03

No cheating

No hacked clients, X-ray, duping, exploits, or anything else that gives you an unfair advantage.

Normal performance and quality-of-life mods are fine, as long as they don't give you an unfair advantage.

04

Keep it appropriate

Chat, usernames, skins, builds, signs, books, and anything else you make need to stay appropriate for a youth group server.

05

Don't harass people

Joking around and messing with people is fine.

Repeatedly targeting, insulting, threatening, or deliberately trying to make the game miserable for someone is not.

06

PvP

PvP is allowed. Fight people as much as you want.

Don't repeatedly spawn camp world spawn or trap someone indefinitely so they can't reasonably play.

Killing someone a few times is one thing. Making it impossible for them to keep playing is another.

07

Don't lag or crash the server

Don't build machines, use exploits, or set anything up specifically to lag or crash the server.

Large redstone machines, mob farms, and similar builds may be limited if they seriously affect performance.

08

Don't impersonate people

Don't pretend to be another player or a staff member in order to trick people.

09

Don't invite random people

This server is for the youth group and people who have been specifically invited.

If you want someone else added, ask Jack first.

10

Use common sense

Not every possible situation needs its own rule.

The goal is for people to mess around, fight, prank each other, and have fun without ruining the server or someone else's experience.

If you're obviously doing something just to make the server miserable for everyone, staff can step in even when there's no specific rule for it.

How rules get enforced

Depending on what happened, staff may give a warning, undo damage, remove items, temporarily ban someone, or permanently ban someone.

Accidents happen. If you break something or cause a problem by accident, tell someone instead of trying to hide it.

Most things are handled based on intent, how bad it was, and whether it keeps happening after you've been told to stop.

Questions, or want someone added to the server? Ask Jack.
